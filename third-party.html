Food data

Product data is supplied by Open Food Facts and its contributors. The database is licensed under ODbL 1.0; individual database contents under DbCL 1.0; product photographs under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0, subject to applicable third-party rights. The linked product page identifies the source. Photos may be resized or cropped for display.

ZeroCal normalizes source nutrition fields and units for display and caches Open Food Facts product records separately from private journals. The Open Food Facts-derived data remains subject to its original license. Contact GEL SPA for the applicable source data or transformation information under that license. This offer excludes private user journals.

Generic reference nutrition uses USDA FoodData Central, public domain under CC0 1.0. These sources do not endorse ZeroCal or AI-generated estimates.
