How do the trial and renewal work?

ZeroCal Plus is an auto-renewable App Store subscription. Eligible new subscribers receive a 3-day free trial on the monthly or annual plan, followed by the localized price and period shown before purchase. If Apple determines that you are not eligible, no free trial is promised.

Both plans belong to the same subscription group. Changing plans or reinstalling the app does not create a new trial. The trial has a separate, limited AI allowance.

Apple charges your Apple Account and manages renewals. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends if you do not want to be charged, following Apple's cancellation instructions. Cancellation and refund rights vary by location; nothing here limits applicable consumer rights.

What is included in ZeroCal Plus?

An active membership provides the food diary, manual and saved-food logging, product/barcode lookup, progress views and available Health connections, plus a limited AI allowance for new analyses and coaching.

Food and activity data depend on what you enter, your permissions, supported devices and external data sources. A subscription does not guarantee a particular nutrition result, weight change, medical outcome or level of estimation accuracy.

Family sharing is not enabled for these plans. Purchase access is associated with the ZeroCal account used to subscribe.

How does the AI allowance work?

New photo, nutrition-label and text analyses, follow-up clarifications and AI coaching use allowance. More complex requests can use more. The allowance is not a fixed number of scans, and no number of daily AI meals is guaranteed.

Paid allowance resets monthly. For a monthly subscription, it follows your verified paid billing period. An annual plan divides its year into 12 monthly allowance periods, starting from the annual paid term; dates adjust for shorter months. Check the reset date shown in the app. The trial has one separate allowance for its verified trial duration. Unused allowance does not roll over, and an annual purchase does not unlock a year's AI capacity at once.

Daily limits and a one-at-a-time analysis limit apply. Before processing begins, enough allowance must remain for the request to complete. The app may therefore stop an analysis even while the meter shows some allowance left.

Searches, saved-food reuse, manual edits and on-device voice transcription do not use AI generation allowance. Sending the transcribed text for an AI estimate does.

There are no automatic AI overage charges. While your membership remains active, manual logging, saved meals and barcode lookup continue when AI allowance is exhausted.

How do I restore, cancel or get billing help?

Use Restore purchases in ZeroCal Plus while signed in to the original ZeroCal and Apple accounts. Manage or cancel your plan in Apple's subscription settings. Deleting the app or ZeroCal account does not cancel billing.

What should I know about nutrition and Health features?

ZeroCal is for adults aged 18 and over and supports general nutrition and wellness. Estimates, targets, meal ideas and projections are informational and can be wrong. Review portions and ingredients before saving.

ZeroCal is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat conditions. GLP-1 notes do not recommend doses or change treatment. Glucose history may be delayed and is not a glucose alarm. Use your clinician and sensor's own app for medical decisions and alerts.
