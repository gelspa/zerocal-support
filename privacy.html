What happens when I use AI?

Before AI sharing is enabled, ZeroCal asks for permission to send the information needed for your request to OpenAI through ZeroCal's cloud service. Relevant context may include meal records, nutrition totals, preferences and goals. Avoid including other people's information or details that are not needed.

New photo, label and text analyses, follow-up clarifications and AI coaching use cloud processing. Native voice input is transcribed on the iPhone; submitting that description for AI sends the resulting text. Older cloud-transcription clients may send audio for processing.

You can turn sharing off in You → Privacy & your data. This stops new AI requests through that account's setting on this device; it does not retract requests already processed. Manual logging, searches and saved-food reuse remain available under your membership.

AI responses are estimates. Review the food and portion before saving. They are not medical diagnoses or decisions about access to care.

Which Apple Health data leaves my iPhone?

Steps, active energy and weight can be copied into your ZeroCal account after you connect Apple Health. ZeroCal does not write data to Apple Health.

Other supported readings—including glucose, sleep, resting energy, body composition, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, water and workouts—are read for the Health views on this iPhone and are not synced to ZeroCal's cloud. Optional GLP-1/appetite notes and glucose check-in preferences are kept locally, separately for each ZeroCal account.

Use Apple Health's app-permission settings to choose individual data types. Disconnecting in ZeroCal stops new reads for that account, but does not automatically erase information already imported. Deleting your ZeroCal account does not delete the source records in Apple Health.

Health readings may be incomplete or delayed. ZeroCal does not replace your sensor's app, glucose alarms or medical care. Health data is not used for advertising in ZeroCal.

Who processes the information?

  • OpenAI processes information sent for AI features.
  • Apple handles App Store purchases and subscription management, and supplies signed transaction information. Apple and Google also process sign-in if you choose those sign-in options.

  • Support and infrastructure providers process messages and technical records needed to deliver the service.

The current app does not include advertising tracking. Information can also be disclosed when needed to comply with a binding legal obligation or protect the security of the service.

Provider details and international processing: The primary ZeroCal account database is configured in Supabase’s London region. Server functions, AI processing, website delivery and provider support may involve other countries, including the United States. ZeroCal does not promise EU-only processing or zero retention. Service-provider processing is subject to their applicable data-processing terms and safeguards; contact GEL SPA for details or a copy of the safeguards relevant to your request.

How long is information kept?

Your account profile, diary and saved cloud photos are kept to provide your account until you remove them or delete the account. The account-deletion flow removes saved cloud photos and deletes the account and associated records. Cost-enforcement records can remain with their account identifier removed.

Removing your account does not erase Apple's purchase records or copies you exported yourself. Support, security, purchase-compliance and backup records have different retention needs:

Account data remains available while the account exists, unless you remove it. Support correspondence is kept to handle the request and any related dispute or legal obligation. Purchase and security records are kept for access verification, abuse prevention and applicable record-keeping duties. The current service does not set a single automatic deletion period for all security, cost or support records. When an account is deleted, the processing-cost ledger can remain with its account link removed. Provider security logs and backup copies follow the relevant provider retention settings and are not guaranteed to disappear at the same time as the live account. Contact GEL SPA for a data-specific retention or deletion request.

AI requests use OpenAI's API. ZeroCal requests that Responses API output not be stored as retrievable application state, but this does not mean zero provider retention. OpenAI's standard API policy includes abuse-monitoring retention and applicable exceptions. See the provider's data controls; ZeroCal does not promise that every copy disappears immediately.

How can I access, change or delete my data?

You can edit your profile and meals in the app. Use You → Account → Delete account to request permanent deletion of the signed-in account. This action is separate from canceling your Apple subscription; cancel through Apple if you also want billing to stop.

Contact support to request a copy of your account data or exercise applicable access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability rights. We may need to verify that the account belongs to you. Depending on where you live, you may also complain to your data-protection authority. Withdrawing consent does not undo processing that already took place.

The app's export for a previous on-device journal is not a complete export of every cloud record; support handles broader account-data requests.

Why is this processing allowed?

Account, purchase and service operations are needed to provide the membership you request. Security and abuse controls protect the service and its users; some purchase or security records may be needed for legal obligations.

Optional Health access and AI sharing have separate controls. Choosing not to enable them does not grant permission to send that information.

We process account and subscription information to provide the service you request and meet applicable legal obligations. Security and abuse controls support our legitimate interest in protecting the service. Before opening or creating your cloud nutrition journal, ZeroCal asks you to expressly allow GEL SPA to store and process your nutrition and health information, and records the accepted policy version and time for your account. You may decline; the cloud journal stays unavailable without this permission. Apple Health access and AI sharing with OpenAI require separate choices. Sign out to stop new syncing on the device, or use Account to delete your cloud data; contact GEL SPA to withdraw consent or make a data request. Withdrawal does not undo processing that has already taken place.

ZeroCal is designed for adults aged 18 and over. If a child's information has been provided in error, contact us so that we can review and remove it where appropriate.

Does this website use tracking?

These help pages do not set analytics or advertising cookies and contain no analytics scripts. GitHub Pages may receive standard connection information such as IP address, browser and requested page to deliver and secure this site. External links take you to providers with their own policies.

If the site later adds forms, analytics or other processing, this notice must be updated before those features are enabled.
