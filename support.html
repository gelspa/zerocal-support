Quick answers

An estimate looks wrong. What should I do?

Open the meal and check its ingredients and portion. Before saving an analysis, use the clarification option to explain what you ate, how much, and whether a weight is raw or cooked. You can also edit the estimate manually.

Photos cannot reliably reveal hidden oils, exact ingredients or weighed portions. Nutrition labels, measured portions and clear descriptions help. Do not rely on an estimate for medication or clinical decisions.

Why is an analysis taking time or failing?

A complex photo or description can need more processing. A weak connection can also interrupt the request. If the app reports a failure, preserve your description and check the connection before trying again. Manual logging and saved meals are alternatives while AI is unavailable.

If it keeps happening, send support your app version, device model, approximate time and the on-screen error. Include a screenshot only if you are comfortable sharing its contents. Repeated attempts can use AI allowance when processing has already started.

Why did AI stop when some allowance remains?

A new analysis must fit within the available allowance before it starts. A complex request may need more than the amount left. Daily limits also apply.

Open You → ZeroCal Plus for the allowance and reset date. Paid allowance refreshes every 30 days, independently of the monthly or annual billing date. Your saved meals, searches, barcode lookup and manual logging remain available while your membership is active. Read the allowance details.

I paid, but my membership is not showing.

Sign in to the same ZeroCal account you used for the purchase and check that your iPhone uses the Apple Account that made it. Open You → ZeroCal Plus → Restore purchases.

A pending Apple payment or Ask to Buy approval does not grant active access until approved. If restoration still fails, contact support with the order reference and error message. Never send your Apple password. A purchase is tied to its original ZeroCal account; it does not automatically move to a newly created account.

How do I cancel or request a refund?

On iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → ZeroCal, then choose the cancellation option. Apple recommends canceling at least 24 hours before a free trial ends if you do not want to be charged.

Deleting ZeroCal or your ZeroCal account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Apple manages App Store refunds; eligibility depends on the transaction and applicable rights.

Apple Health or glucose history is empty.

Open ZeroCal's Apple Health settings and connect or update access. In Apple Health, check the data types shared with ZeroCal and whether the source app has recorded any readings.

Apple keeps read permissions private, so an empty result does not tell ZeroCal whether access was denied or no reading exists. A glucose sensor's own app must share readings with Apple Health, and those readings may arrive later. Use the sensor's app for current readings and medical alerts.

How do I stop AI sharing or delete my account?

Open You → Privacy & your data to turn off AI sharing. Manage individual Health permissions in Apple Health.

For deletion, open You → Account → Delete account. Review which account is signed in first. Deletion is permanent and does not cancel Apple billing. If you cannot sign in, contact support for account recovery or a verified data request. Read the privacy notice.

For medical concerns, contact a healthcare professional. ZeroCal support cannot diagnose symptoms, change treatment or provide emergency monitoring.
